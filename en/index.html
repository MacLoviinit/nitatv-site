What NitaTV does

  • Sign in with an M3U playlist link or with your provider's credentials
  • Add your playlist from your phone by scanning a QR code
  • Live TV with a program guide
  • Movies and series, resuming where you left off
  • Record a show, right away or scheduled from the guide
  • Download movies and episodes to watch offline
  • Cast to a Chromecast device
  • Choose the audio track and subtitles
  • Several profiles, favorites and search
  • Picture-in-picture
  • Available in 21 languages

Frequently asked questions

Does NitaTV provide channels?

No. NitaTV is a player: it plays what your provider gives you. It contains no channels and recommends no provider.

What do I need?

An M3U playlist link, or the credentials your provider gave you. You can type them on the TV, or send them from your phone by scanning a QR code.

Which devices?

Android TV and Google TV, Android tablets and phones. From a phone or a tablet, you can also cast to a Chromecast.

How much does it cost?

A free one-month trial, no credit card needed, one per Google account. Then a yearly subscription, cancel anytime from Google Play.

What happens to my credentials?

They stay on your device, in an encrypted vault. When sent from your phone by QR code, they are end-to-end encrypted: our server cannot read them. NitaTV contains no ads and no trackers.
